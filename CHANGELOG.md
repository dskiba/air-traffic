## Unreleased

First public version.

- Routes links by domain, domain + path, and source app; picker at the cursor when no rule matches.
- `⌃⌥Space` launcher to type or paste a link, or search.
- Browser profiles for Chrome, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi, Chromium, Firefox and Zen; private windows.
- Strips trackers, unwraps redirect links, opens Zoom/Teams/Figma/Spotify/Notion/Linear in their apps.
- Settings: default browser, open at login, menu bar icon, rules, hidden browsers.
- Universal app, macOS 26+, under 1 MB, no dependencies, no network access.
